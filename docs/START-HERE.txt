☣️ BLACKSWARM // RAINBOW SKULL SWARM 🌈

A free-first public-index research tool from GlitchByte Phantom Labs.

QUICK START (Python 3):
  bash RUN-BLACKSWARM.sh --color always

CHOOSE YOUR PATH:
  [1]  LIVE SEARCH: Enter a topic, choose public sources, link cap and quality.
  [11] CONSTELLATION NAVIGATOR: Browse old results; use [5] QUICK FIND.
  [12] BOOKMARKED INTEL: Star and note interesting public links.
  [13] INTEL COLLECTIONS: Organize bookmarks into named collections and tags.
  [20] SWARM COCKPIT: Open the working local graphical research interface.

OPEN COCKPIT DIRECTLY:
  bash OPEN-SWARM-COCKPIT.sh

The visual interface is local at 127.0.0.1, and never opens automatically
when simply starting the rainbow terminal. Keep private vaults on your machine.
