☣ BLACKSWARM // QUICK START 🌈

BLACKSWARM is a terminal-first public-index research tool from GlitchByte Phantom Labs.

Run from the repository directory (Python 3 required):
  bash RUN-BLACKSWARM.sh --color always

Choose [1] LIVE PUBLIC-INDEX SEARCH to begin.
Choose [11] CONSTELLATION NAVIGATOR to browse saved links.
Choose [12] BOOKMARKED INTEL to manage favorites and notes.

The browser stays closed until you intentionally open the optional local dashboard.
All research data and exports stay on your computer.

See the full guide in MANUAL.md or the project home in ../README.md.
